Deprecated All Team Drive-related fields are removed from the Vault API. Although these fields are marked as deprecated and will continue to be available until then, we strongly recommend that you update your apps and your API configuration to reference the new fields as soon as possible. If you do not migrate your apps by June 1, 2020, they might stop functioning.
