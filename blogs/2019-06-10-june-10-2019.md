Change The "Team Drive" feature of Google Drive has been renamed to "shared drive". Consequently, the Vault API is changing as follows: Shared drive-related request and response fields have replaced Team Drive in: DriveOptions HeldDriveQuery SearchMethod SharedDriveInfo has replaced TeamDriveInfo .
